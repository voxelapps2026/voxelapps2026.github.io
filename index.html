MindGym: Think Without AI

Your brain is the only tool here. No AI, no autocomplete — just you.

MindGym is a daily cognitive-fitness app for people who live in an AI-heavy world. It trains the skills that atrophy when thinking gets outsourced: memory, attention, and reasoning. There is no generative AI anywhere in the app — puzzles are procedurally generated, so they are effectively endless and never repeat a fixed bank.

What's inside

  • 16 games across memory, attention, and reasoning, each scored 0–100
  • A daily workout that adapts its difficulty to how you're performing
  • Focus sessions with an ongoing timer, plus a reflection journal
  • Progress tracking — streaks, per-skill trends, and average response time
  • Friend duels: pure score comparison, no stakes and no wagering

Pricing

Everything is free and supported by ads. A single one-time purchase of $3.99 removes the ads. There is no subscription, and no feature is ever locked behind payment.
